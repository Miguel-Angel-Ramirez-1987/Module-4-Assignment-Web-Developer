Layer 2: Directly Connecting to Ethereum's' Mainnet.

As ethereum grow the need for distinguished ecosystems materialized. Layer 2 Rollups are blockchains developed ontop of Ethereum's' Mainnet. Layer 2 Rollups have a direct connection to Ethereum's' Mainnet. Layer 2 Ecosystems are blockchains developed ontop of Ethereum's' Layer 2 Rollups. Layer 2 Ecosystems have a direct connection to Ethereum's' Mainnet via the Layer 2 Rollups.

It is important to know that Blockchains that are built separate from Ethereum must communicate through a smart contract via a Layer 2. So other blockchain such as Bitcoin or Solana must affirm a contract with a Layer 2 Solution before transacting with Ethereum.
