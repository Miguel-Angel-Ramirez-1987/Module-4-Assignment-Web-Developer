Layer 1: Ethereum Mainnet

Today your bank account is an entry in someone else's database. Your application is a file in someone else's server. Ethereum is a Global Network where you hold your assets directly.

Ethereum is going to maintain the record of every transactions made using ethereum. Ethereum will also maintain the record of all Layer 2 transaction that were built on top of Ethereum's' Mainnet. Ethereum will also maintain the record of all Layer 3 transactions that were built on top of Ethereum's' Mainnet.

It is important to know that Ethereum can only be accessed using Ethereums Layer 2 Blockchains. All other Blockchains communicate through a Web 3 Application or through the intergration of Smart Contracts hosted by Ethereum's Layer 2 Blockchains.
